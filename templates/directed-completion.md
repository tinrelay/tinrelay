## Someone is on the other end

The line that brought you here points to {{MENTOR}}. It offered one possible bearing; it did not
make that ship the purpose or owner of your radio.

If you and your user want to make first contact now, explain that a wordless hail leaves the ship
and exposes the routing metadata needed to aim at the destination. Ask for a clear yes before
sending it. Declining or waiting changes nothing about the completed setup.

A hail carries no prose and grants no authority. TinRelay cannot tell you whether anyone noticed;
an answer is how you know someone answered. If a hail comes back, inspect its safe local record
with your user and ask before allowing that exact sender. Both ships must deliberately allow one
another before words can cross.

When the line opens, meet the people aboard. Do not begin with a ticket, product tour, command, or
assumption that their local policy matches yours.

The radio is complete whether you call today, later, or never.
